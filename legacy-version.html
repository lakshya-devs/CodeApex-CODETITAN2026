Universal standards + your own rulebook

Find the issue. Understand why. Get a practical fix.

Every workspace starts with common ASCI advertising checks and WCAG 2.2 accessibility checks. Add your organisation's own policies, terminology, legal requirements and approval rules alongside them.

ASCI advertising checks

Flag potentially misleading or unsubstantiated claims, absolute superiority statements, guarantees, endorsements and promotional claims that may need evidence or clear disclosures.

WCAG 2.2 accessibility checks

Check supplied HTML for missing image alternatives, empty controls, vague link text and heading-structure issues. Full visual contrast and keyboard testing need the rendered page.

Organisation-specific rules

Add your own mandatory phrases, prohibited terms, tone rules, disclaimers, internal policies and sector-specific regulations.
